# Dune Arcanum artwork

Five self-contained SVG illustrations share a 480 × 720 viewBox. They contain no text, external resources, fonts, scripts, or filters.

- **sand-gate.svg** — warm sandstone, muted turquoise, copper; quiet central field with an arched gateway emerging at the lower edges.
- **lapis-court.svg** — lapis, aquamarine, copper; ceramic side columns and a courtyard reflecting pool.
- **glass-observatory.svg** — midnight indigo, pale glass, brass; sparse stars, twin observatory domes, and an armillary sphere.
- **atlas-vault.svg** — charcoal violet, deep teal, gold; faceted vault edges and an astronomical floor seal.
- **mosaic.svg** — restored atlas, richer lapis/teal/gold; twelve interlocking rosettes, graduated astronomical rings, constellations, and a central compass flower.

Chapter backgrounds intentionally keep the middle 340 pixels subdued for gameplay. Preserve their aspect ratio; place bright targets and trajectory strokes above them. The mosaic is suitable as a full reveal, cropped level-completion detail, or a tile-masked restoration layer.
