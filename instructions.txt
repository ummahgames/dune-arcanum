# “Dune Arcanum” — Brick Breaker (No Paddle) Game Design (Markdown)

## 1) High-level Concept

A one-finger/one-mouse **trajectory brick breaker** with no paddle. You **launch** a ball from wherever it lands. After every launch resolves (i.e., the ball stops moving), **all bricks descend** by a small step. If any brick crosses the **ground line**, you lose. The fantasy is “desert–Islamic geometry”: tessellated patterns, brass inlay, star polygons, tiling mosaics, wind-blown sand, and calligraphic accents.

---

## 2) Core Loop

1. **Aim**: Drag to choose an angle from the ball’s current rest position.
2. **Launch**: Release to fire; ball travels with elastic collisions off walls and bricks.
3. **Resolve**: Ball bounces until it falls to rest (y ≤ floor + ε or velocity ≈ 0 and below a threshold height).
4. **Descent**: All bricks descend by `rowStep` pixels/units.
5. **Check**: If any brick’s bottom ≥ ground → **Game Over**; else **repeat**.

---

## 3) Controls & UX

* **Mouse/Touch**: Press/drag to aim; release to launch.
* **Aim Guide**: Predictive dotted line with limited bounces (e.g., up to 3 visualized) that respects wall reflections and first-collision with bricks.
* **Fast-forward**: Tap/space to increase simulation time scale while the ball is in flight.
* **Undo (optional power-up)**: Re-aim the last shot before descent phase (limited uses).
* **Settings**: Sound toggles, vibration, color-blind friendly palettes, reduced FX.

---

## 4) World & Coordinate System

* Origin at **bottom-left**. `x ∈ [0, W]`, `y ∈ [0, H]`. Ground at `y = 0`.
* **Walls**: Left `x=0`, Right `x=W`, Ceiling `y=H`.
* **Ball**: radius `r`. Initial speed `v0`. Gravity **off** (classic brick breaker feel) or **very light** (optional mode).
* **Time step**: Fixed Δt (e.g., 1/120 s) with accumulator for deterministic physics.

---

## 5) Ball Physics & Collisions

* **Motion**: `p(t+Δt) = p(t) + v(t) * Δt`.
* **Walls**: Perfectly elastic:

  * Left/Right: `vx = -vx`; Ceiling: `vy = -vy`; Floor: landing occurs when `y ≤ r`.
* **Bricks**: AABB or convex polygons. On hit:

  * Compute **time-of-impact** against AABB; reflect velocity across contact normal:

    * `v' = v - 2 * (v · n) * n` where `n` is the collision normal.
  * Apply brick damage; spawn particles; continue trajectory within the same frame using **swept** collision resolution to prevent tunneling.
* **Corner cases**: Use small positional slop (`ε`) and **continuous collision detection** (raycast from previous to next position).

---

## 6) Bricks & Entities

### Brick Types

* **Basic Tile (1 HP)**: Single hit.
* **Carved Tile (2–3 HP)**: HP indicated by geometric overlay ring count.
* **Sandstone Block (High HP, Heavy)**: Larger, fewer per row.
* **Obsidian (Reflector)**: Does not break; reflects with +10% speed (cap at `vMax`).
* **Mirror Triangle**: Reflects changing angle slightly (adds curvature by modifying normal over area).
* **Explosive Urn**: On break, radial damage to neighbors.
* **Prism**: Splits ball into 2 weaker balls with 50% damage for the remaining flight (optional).
* **Rune Gate**: Teleporter pair; preserves speed; exits with small angular jitter.
* **Crumbling Sand**: When hit, leaves a small falling sand FX, but still counts as a break.

### Special Rows

* **“Calligraphy Chain”**: Linked HP; breaking one propagates 1 damage along the chain.

---

## 7) Descent Mechanic

* After each launch resolves, **all bricks descend** by `rowStep` (e.g., 12 px).
* New bricks spawn at the **ceiling** aligned to a row grid after N turns or per level script.
* **Speed pressure**: Later levels increase `rowStep` or spawn rows more frequently.

---

## 8) Power-Ups (on collision or end-of-turn rewards)

* **Pierce**: First N bricks ignore bounce and take full damage.
* **Multiball**: Spawn M extra balls with slight angular offsets (re-merge on landing, optional).
* **Ricochet+**: +1 to max bounces visualized and slight elasticity boost.
* **Time Shard**: Temporarily slows time (except input).
* **Sandstorm**: On next descent, pushes all bricks back up by half a step (stackable? limit 1).
* **Bomb**: Next hit triggers small AoE.
* **Blessing of Symmetry**: Next shot mirrors a ghost shot at the symmetric angle relative to vertical.
* **Rune of Return**: If a block would touch the ground this turn, revert last rowStep once (consumed).

Power-ups can drop from special tiles or be **earned** via score thresholds.

---

## 9) Scoring & Progression

* **Per-hit score** scaled by brick HP + combo multiplier for consecutive hits without walls.
* **Chain bonus**: +X% per brick in a single flight, resets on landing.
* **Perfect Descent**: If after descent no bricks are within `dangerBand` of ground, +bonus.
* **Level Goals**: Clear all blocks **or** survive K descents.
* **Worlds**: Each world tweaks parameters (rowStep, density, brick mix) and visual theme variants.

---

## 10) Level Design & Generation

### Hand-Crafted

* Use **Islamic tiling layouts**: girih (decagon, pentagon, bow-tie), star polygons (8-, 10-, 12-point), kufic letter patterns. Form **symmetry groups** (p6m, p4m).
* Place challenge elements (reflectors, prisms) in symmetric constellations.

### Procedural

* Seeded RNG. Define **row blueprints** as strings:

  * `"B2 . U P . B1"` (B2=brick HP2, U=urn, P=prism, .=empty).
* Difficulty curve:

  * Increase **HP bands**, **row density**, **reflector ratio**, **spawn cadence**.
* **Boss Rows**: Large HP glyph that requires multi-angle shots through small apertures.

---

## 11) Failure & Win Conditions

* **Lose**: Any brick’s bottom ≥ ground after descent.
* **Win**: No bricks remain **or** you complete objective turns.

---

## 14) Technology & Implementation (Client-side HTML/JS/CSS/WebGL)

### Stack

* **Rendering**: WebGL 2 (via raw GL or a light engine). Canvas 2D fallback.
* **Physics**: Custom lightweight (swept AABB) or matter-js (if 2D) with tuned restitution (but custom is simpler).
* **Architecture**: ECS (Entity–Component–System) for bricks, ball, power-ups, effects.

### Performance Targets

* **60 FPS** mobile & desktop.
* Batched **instanced** rendering for bricks; per-instance uniforms for HP colorization.
* Single-pass particle system using GPU sprites; frustum cull offscreen rows.

---

## 15) Rendering Details

* **Bricks**: Instanced quads (position, size, HP, typeId).
* **Material**:

  * BaseColor map (ceramic), **normal map** for carved grooves,
  * Metallic/Roughness for brass frames,
  * **Environment reflection** for obsidian (cheap cube-map or screen-space approximation).
* **Girih Overlay**:

  * Full-screen tiled shader (UV → quasiperiodic mask) or precomputed pattern atlas.
  * Multiply/blend over bricks for ornate look.
* **Aim Guide**: Line strip with fading alpha; small star glyphs at predicted bounces.
* **Post FX (optional)**: Subtle vignette; heat-haze for desert shimmer near ground (distortion pass).

## 18) Core Systems (Pseudo-code)

### Launch & Resolve

```js
function launch(angle) {
  ball.vx = Math.cos(angle) * v0;
  ball.vy = Math.sin(angle) * v0;
  state = "inFlight";
}

function update(dt) {
  if (state !== "inFlight") return;

  // Integrate
  let nextPos = ball.pos.add(ball.vel.mul(dt));

  // Continuous collision detection against walls
  handleWallSwept(ball, nextPos);

  // Raycast against bricks, earliest time-of-impact
  let hit = earliestBrickHit(ball.pos, nextPos, bricks);
  if (hit) {
    // Move to impact
    ball.pos = hit.point;
    // Damage brick
    applyDamage(hit.brick, damageFor(ball));
    // Reflect
    ball.vel = reflect(ball.vel, hit.normal);
    // Nudge out along normal
    ball.pos = ball.pos.add(hit.normal.mul(epsilon));
  } else {
    ball.pos = nextPos;
  }

  // Landing check
  if (ball.pos.y - ball.radius <= groundY && ball.vel.y < 0 && speed(ball.vel) < landSpeed) {
    ball.pos.y = groundY + ball.radius;
    state = "landed";
    beginDescentPhase();
  }
}
```

### Descent Phase

```js
function beginDescentPhase() {
  // move all bricks down
  for (let b of bricks) b.y -= rowStep;

  // spawn new row at top if rule matches
  if (turn % spawnEveryTurns === 0) spawnRowAtTop();

  // game over check
  for (let b of bricks) {
    if (b.y - b.h/2 <= groundY) gameOver();
  }
  state = "aiming"; // new turn
  turn++;
}
```

### Aim Preview (limited bounces)

```js
function buildAimPreview(origin, dir, maxBounces=3) {
  let points = [origin];
  let p = origin, v = dir.norm().mul(previewLen);
  for (let i=0;i<maxBounces;i++){
    let hit = raycastWallsAndFirstBrick(p, v);
    if (!hit) break;
    points.push(hit.point);
    v = reflect(v, hit.normal);
    p = hit.point.add(hit.normal.mul(epsilon));
  }
  return points;
}
```

## 19) Collision Details (Swept AABB)

* Treat ball as circle; expand brick AABBs by radius; cast a **ray** from `p` to `p + v*dt`.
* For each brick, compute entry/exit times `tEntry`, `tExit` along x/y; collide if `tEntry ≤ tExit` and `0 ≤ tEntry ≤ 1`; pick **smallest `tEntry`**.
* Normal is axis of maximum `tEntry` (x or y); reflect accordingly.

## 23) WebGL Notes

* **Geometry**: Brick quad VBO; instance attributes: `a_pos`, `a_size`, `a_hp`, `a_type`.
* **Vertex Shader**: Transform to NDC; pass UV & instance data.
* **Fragment Shader**:

  * Sample **pattern atlas**; modulate by HP (saturation/brightness).
  * Optional **screen-space normal** perturbation for ceramic glaze.
* **Heat-Haze**: Fullscreen pass with noise-based UV offset near ground band.
* **Particles**: GPU sprites with a single draw call (texture atlas of sand/spark).

---

## 24) Content Pipeline

* **Pattern Atlases**: Prebaked PNGs of girih motifs at multiple scales.
* **SDF Fonts** for crisp kufic headers at any resolution.
* **JSON** level files; load via `fetch`; validate against schema.
* **Localization**: RTL support for Arabic via CSS `direction: rtl` in text regions.

## 25) Tuning & Difficulty

* Early levels: fewer descents, low HP, generous gaps.
* Mid: introduce reflectors, urns, prisms; `rowStep` increases.
* Late: narrow angles required, anti-cheese (e.g., dampen repeated wall skims), more teleports.

---

## 26) Analytics (optional)

* Track **angle histograms**, **turns survived**, **shots per clear**, **power-up usage**.
* Use to auto-adjust suggested angles (teach moments) or dynamic difficulty.

## 27) QA Edge Cases

* Ball trapped bouncing horizontally → **angle jitter** after N wall hits.
* Multiple simultaneous hits → resolve by earliest `tEntry`; queue others next frame.
* Teleport exit inside brick → push along exit normal until free (safety solver).

## 30) Quick Balancing Defaults

* `rowStep = 12 px`, `spawnEveryTurns = 1`.
* `v0 = 900 px/s`, clamp aim angle to `[10°, 170°]` to avoid dead dribbles.
* Aim preview length ≈ 1.5× screen diagonal; max 3 bounces visualized.
* Early HP range 1–3; reflectors ≤10% of bricks.
